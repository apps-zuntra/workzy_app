Workzi Privacy Policy

How Workzi collects, uses, and safeguards personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

Effective Date: October 2026 • Publisher: Appszuntra / Zuntra • Jurisdiction: India
This Privacy Policy describes how Workzi, operated by Appszuntra, a subsidiary of Zuntra ("Company"), collects, uses, and protects personal data of users of the Workzi platform. It is published in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and applicable rules.

Workzi is a workforce management platform that brings attendance, workforce scheduling, team coordination, activity visibility, and operational processes into one connected platform for organizations. This Policy covers the Workzi web and mobile applications and website.

1 Definitions

1.1 "Personal Data": Any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data, as defined under the DPDP Act.
1.2 "Data Principal": You, the individual whose Personal Data is processed, including an Employee or an Administrator using the Platform.
1.3 "Data Fiduciary": The person who determines the purpose and means of processing Personal Data. For Workforce Data, this is the Customer. For account, billing, and website data, this is the Company.
1.4 "Data Processor": The person who processes Personal Data on behalf of a Data Fiduciary. The Company acts as Data Processor for Workforce Data on the Customer's instructions.
1.5 "Customer": The organization that subscribes to Workzi, usually your employer.
1.6 "Workforce Data": Attendance, schedule, leave, location check-in, activity, and related records generated by or about Employees through the Platform.
1.7 "Employee" / "Authorized User": An individual whom a Customer has authorized to use the Platform, including managers, HR teams, and Administrators.

2 Scope

This Policy applies to Personal Data collected through Workzi from Customers' Administrators and Employees, and from visitors to the Workzi website. Your employer's own policies on attendance and monitoring are separate, and your employer is responsible for them.

4 Sensitive Data: Location and Verification

📍
Location Transparency
Location coordinates are collected only at the exact moments of check-in and check-out or within designated office geofences to validate attendance presence. Workzi does not conduct background surveillance or continuous 24/7 personal tracking without active user initiation.
4.1 Location data is collected strictly at the time of check-in or check-out, or as explicitly configured by your employer and disclosed to you.
4.2 Verification data (such as check-in photo confirmation) is used exclusively to confirm identity during punch events, is secured with high-grade cloud encryption, and is not utilized for commercial behavioral profiling.
4.3 Workforce Data is strictly visible only to authorized Administrators and managers of your own organization according to role-based access controls. It is never accessible or shared with other Customers.

5 How We Use Your Information

  • Create and maintain secure accounts and authenticate authorized users
  • Record attendance, manage shifts, calculate leaves, and generate analytics for your employer
  • Verify punch events using geofencing and anti-spoofing location verification
  • Provide customer technical support and send transactional operational notifications
  • Process organizational subscription billing and compliance invoices
  • Improve and secure Platform resilience using aggregated or de-identified diagnostics
  • Detect and prevent fraud, system abuse, and credential compromises
  • Comply with applicable legal and statutory labor obligations

Note: We strictly never use Workforce Data for third-party advertising or commercial monetization.

6 Consent & Legal Grounds

6.1 By registering or using the Platform, you acknowledge the processing described in this Policy. Where your employer is the Data Fiduciary, your employer is responsible for providing notice and obtaining employee consent or establishing employment-related statutory processing grounds under the DPDP Act.
6.2 You may withdraw consent for optional permissions (such as location services or camera check-ins) through your device settings or by contacting your organization's administrator.

7 Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your Personal Data. We share it only:

  • With your employer: As the Customer, to the extent necessary to provide workforce management features.
  • With verified service providers: Secure cloud infrastructure (Google Firebase / Google Cloud), database hosting, push notifications, and payment gateways.
  • With regulators or law enforcement: Where strictly required by court orders or binding Indian law.
  • Corporate restructuring: In connection with a merger or acquisition, subject to identical confidentiality protections.

8 Data Retention & Security

We retain Personal Data as long as necessary for the operational purposes described here, as instructed by the Customer, or as required by Indian statutory labor laws. Following termination of a subscription, data is permanently purged or anonymized in accordance with agreement terms.

We maintain rigorous industry safeguards, including TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, strict IAM role isolation, and security audit logs in compliance with the IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.
